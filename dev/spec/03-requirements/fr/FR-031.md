## Requirement

- **Statement:** When a follow-up request leaves unclear which slides it reaches, or what to change, the system changes no slide and asks one question, which ends the request. For an unclear scope it offers suggested answers such as every slide, the named slides or new slides only; for an unclear change it offers directions taken from the deck and the conversation, such as the content, the readability or the layout of a slide. With no direction to offer, it asks what to change.
- **Status:** confirmed

- Legacy FR-030 (change no slide while a change is unclear) and FR-035 (clarify feedback that does not say what to change) are merged here.
- Changed vs legacy: the legacy request stayed open while it waited for the answer. A question now ends the request, and the reply is a new request (FR-014). Legacy FR-032, FR-033 and FR-034 (outcomes chosen within the waiting request) are dropped.
