## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the saved work of a chosen chat cannot be read, the system says that it cannot open that chat, and why when it knows, and names no cause it does not know, such as a deletion. It shows no empty chat in its place, removes no chat, and keeps that chat among the earlier chats as one it cannot open. Every other chat, and the chat the user was in, stay as they were.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- A project folder that cannot be read is a different failure (legacy FR-074), reviewed with legacy UC-013.
