## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the request or an interview answer asks for presenter notes, the system writes the notes of each slide with that slide, in the language of the deck. The notes hold what the presenter says, not a copy of the slide text. The closing message names the slides with notes, or says that the generation format of the deck holds no notes.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** The duration is an interview topic; notes help the presenter keep to it. The PO ruled on 04-11-2026 to keep presenter notes.
