updated: 04-11-2026

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the system shows a revised outline, it names each planned slide it added, removed, moved or changed, and each item of the brief it changed, compared with the outline proposal it revised. This includes each change that the revision did not ask for.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** Napkin AI was observed changing more slides than a revision said (legacy research `dev/legacy/research/use-cases/napkin-ai/napkin-ai-deck-generation-behavior.md`); the PO ruled on 04-11-2026 that each change is named.
