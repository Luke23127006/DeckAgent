## Requirement

- **Statement:** While DeckAgent runs, reloading or closing its interface, a lost connection between the interface and DeckAgent, going to another chat, or switching the workspace in use, does not stop an active request. When the user comes back, the system shows the chat as it is now: the conversation, the deck, the activities and what the system is doing now. It shows no notice that the work was interrupted while the work goes on.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- Legacy UC-003 A12 (connection lost) is merged into this behavior. A provider that is unreachable is a request that does not finish (FR-019).
- Legacy FR-054 (change of chat or of the workspace in use while a request runs) is merged here (05-11-2026).
