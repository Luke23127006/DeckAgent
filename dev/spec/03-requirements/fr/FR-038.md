## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system keeps the draft of each chat while the user is elsewhere in the product, such as in another chat or in setup, and shows it unchanged when the user comes back. Going elsewhere adds nothing to the draft and sends nothing.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- Rewritten from legacy "Keep the chat while a place for setup or tools is open". The list of places is dropped: links to other places are not flows.
- The draft is not kept after DeckAgent stops (owner ruling of 05-11-2026, UC-006 review).
- Legacy FR-111 (keep the pending request during design setup) is merged here on 05-11-2026.
