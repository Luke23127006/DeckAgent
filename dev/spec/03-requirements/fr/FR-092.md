## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system does not finish a slide it builds or changes while the slide has known missing required content, wrong facts or unreadable content [NFR-009]. It corrects the fault within the active request. When it cannot correct the fault, it ends the request unfinished.
- **Status:** confirmed
