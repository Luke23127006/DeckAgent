## Rule

- **Statement:** The rule sets the topics of the interview before the first outline, and the kinds of source a deck draws from.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** Teacher feedback of 23-10-2026: a fixed list that skips what the request already answers.

## Values

### V-1 · Interview topics

- **Value:** Closed list: topic, audience, duration, purpose, sources, language, images, generation format, template, design system.

### V-2 · Source kinds

- **Value:** Closed list: the user's material, the model's general knowledge, public research.

## Notes

- Hard constraints, such as content that must be included or a word limit, are not a topic. The user adds them as other requirements in their own words.
