id: BR-014
title: "Licences of images from public research"
---

## Rule

- **Statement:** The rule sets which use licences allow an image found by public research on a slide.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** The use of a deck is not known in advance, and a layout can crop or change an image, so only licences that allow any use and any change, asking at most for attribution, are accepted.

## Values

### V-1 · Accepted licences

- **Value:** Closed list: public domain, CC0, CC BY.

### V-2 · Licences not accepted

- **Value:** Every other licence, including CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC and CC BY-ND.
