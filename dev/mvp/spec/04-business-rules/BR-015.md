title: "Content that needs a source"
---

## Rule

- **Statement:** The rule sets the kinds of content that the system writes only from the user's material or from public research, never from the model's general knowledge alone.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** A made-up figure or name is the error a reader of an AI deck is least able to spot; the wording around it is safe for the model to write.

## Values

### V-1 · Kinds of content that need a source

- **Value:** Closed list: figures and statistics; dates; names of people and organisations; quotations; results and claims about the user's own work or project.

## Notes

- Wording, transitions and examples marked as examples need no source.
