id: NFR-006
title: "Keys kept out of the work and its records"
---

## Requirement

- **Quality attribute:** security
- **Statement:** A **saved key reaches only the parts of DeckAgent** that **send requests to its own provider or outside service**. It is **never part of what the model receives**, and **never appears** in a conversation, a deck, a record of an error or **any file outside the place where DeckAgent keeps keys**.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- **How and where DeckAgent keeps a key**, and **whether it encrypts it**, is **runtime research** of the first build that touches models.
