title: "Slide display integrity"
---

## Requirement

- **Quality attribute:** display integrity
- **Statement:** Each finished slide shows in full in the deck canvas. Its text is not cut off and does not run outside the slide, and no content covers other content so that it cannot be read. Each image, font and other resource the slide uses shows, and its characters show correctly in the language of the deck.
- **Status:** confirmed
