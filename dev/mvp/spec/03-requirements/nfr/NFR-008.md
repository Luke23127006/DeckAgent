---
id: NFR-008
title: "Saved state after an unexpected end"
---

## Requirement

- **Quality attribute:** data integrity
- **Statement:** When **DeckAgent ends unexpectedly**, the **last saved state** of each chat, its deck and the setup of providers and models **is kept**. After DeckAgent **starts again**, each chat **opens in its last saved state** and **accepts a new request**. **No unfinished change to a slide shows as a finished result**.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- It makes **no promise that a result shown just before the end was saved**. The **draft of a chat is not covered**.
