---
id: FR-125
title: "Licence and source of images from public research"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system uses no image found by public research whose licence is known and not accepted [BR-014]. It marks an image whose licence is unknown. The closing message names the source, the author and the licence of each image found by public research.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- The licence check is separate from the choice of image (FR-066).
