id: FR-130
title: "Write or change presenter notes when asked"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the request or an interview answer asks for presenter notes, the system writes the notes of each slide with that slide, and when a follow-up request asks to add or change the notes of named slides, it writes the notes of those slides only, in the language of the deck. The notes hold what the presenter says, not a copy of the slide text, and writing them changes nothing the audience sees. The closing message names the slides with new notes, or says that the generation format of the deck holds no notes.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** The duration is an interview topic; notes help the presenter keep to it.
