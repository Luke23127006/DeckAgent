title: "Summarize a conversation that outgrows the context of the model"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the **conversation of a chat no longer fits the context** of the selected model during a request, the system **writes a conversation summary** in place of the earlier conversation, **goes on with the work**, and **says in the conversation that it did so** and **where the summary starts**. The summary **replaces only the conversation the model reads**: later requests **still use the brief, the confirmed outline, the deck and the attachments in full**, and the **user still reads the whole conversation**. When the system **cannot write the summary**, the **request does not finish** (as UC-003 A11) and the **conversation stays as it was**.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- The user has **no compact command**. **Who starts the summary**: [DEC-008](../../../decisions/DEC-008-who-starts-a-conversation-summary.md).
- Whether **OpenCode keeps the earlier messages readable** after a compaction is **not documented**; **runtime research of the first build checks it** ([dev/mvp/research/context-compaction.md](../../../research/context-compaction.md) item 3).
