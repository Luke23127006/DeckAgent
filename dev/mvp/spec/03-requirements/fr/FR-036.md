id: FR-036
title: "Refuse a slide reference that does not match the deck"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When a follow-up request **names a slide that is not in the deck**, or gives a **slide number and a slide title** that **point to different slides**, the system **changes no slide**. It says **which part of the request does not match** the deck and **how many slides the deck has**. It **keeps the request** in the conversation and **does not send it again** by itself.
- **Status:** confirmed
