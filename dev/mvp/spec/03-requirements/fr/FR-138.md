title: "Keep and name the source of content that needs one"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When it **writes content of a kind that needs a source** [BR-015] on a slide, the system **keeps its source**: the file and the place in the user's material, the public research result, or the model's general knowledge. When **asked where content of the deck comes from**, it **names the kept source**, or says that it **does not know the source when none was kept**. It **never presents a named source as proof** that the content is true.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** The **source is kept when the content is written**, **not found again when asked**.
