id: FR-048
title: "Show the earlier chats"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the user **asks for earlier work**, the system **shows the earlier chats of the workspace in use** that the user is allowed to open [BR-004], **including chats from before DeckAgent last started**, and **shows the ungrouped chats apart from them**, **whichever workspace is in use**. It **names each chat from its content**, and says **when the chat last changed** and **whether it has slides**. With **no such chat** it **says so**, and the **current chat and its draft stay** as they were.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- A **chat of a removed workspace becomes an ungrouped chat** (FR-073).
