title: "Show the earlier chats"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the user asks for earlier work, the system shows the earlier chats of the workspace in use that the user is allowed to open [BR-004], including chats from before DeckAgent last started. It names each chat from its content, and says when the chat last changed and whether it has slides. With no such chat it says so, and the current chat and its draft stay as they were.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- The chats of a removed workspace are chats of the default workspace (FR-073).
