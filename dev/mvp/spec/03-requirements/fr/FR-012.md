---
id: FR-012
title: "Send a request and show its work until it ends"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the user **sends a request**, the system **moves its request text and attachments** into the conversation as the **user's message**, and **shows at once that it works on it** [NFR-001]. While the request is active, the **status message** says in plain words **what the system is doing** and **how long it has worked**. When the **work ends**, the system **says so** and **accepts a new request**.
- **Status:** confirmed
