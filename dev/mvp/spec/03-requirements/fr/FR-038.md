---
id: FR-038
title: "Keep the draft of a chat"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system keeps the draft of each chat while the user is elsewhere in the product, such as in another chat or in setup, and shows it unchanged when the user comes back. Going elsewhere adds nothing to the draft and sends nothing.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- The draft is not kept after DeckAgent stops.
