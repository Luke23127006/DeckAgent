id: FR-096
title: "Show a proposed design system before it is saved"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** **Before a design system is saved**, the system **shows each theme value, each rule in words and each brand file** of the proposed design system [BR-017], with a **sample slide** made with it. It **names each rule it cannot use**, with the reason, and **each two rules that do not agree**, with where each came from, and it **chooses neither** of two rules that do not agree. **Showing a proposed design system changes no deck**.
- **Status:** confirmed

## Notes

- A design system holds **theme values and rules in words**, with **no Required or Preferred Rules**: **code checks the theme values** (FR-092), and the **rules in words are judged**.
- A new design system **starts from a complete one** (FR-097), so **no minimum content is checked**.
- **Rules of density, headings, hierarchy, charts and images** are **rules in words** of a design system.
