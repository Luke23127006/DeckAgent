id: FR-131
title: "Aim a request at a marked part of a slide"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The user is allowed to **mark one or more parts of slides** in the deck canvas **as the target of a request**. **Each marked part goes with the request** and **shows with it in the conversation**. The system **takes the marked parts as the scope of the change** and **changes nothing else on those slides** unless the request asks for it.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** Teacher feedback of 23-10-2026: the user of an HTML deck **edits by drawing on what they see**, and the **mark goes into the conversation for the agent**. Reference product: **Claude slide design**.
