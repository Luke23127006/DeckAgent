title: "Send a suggested answer or a suggested revision"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** **Choosing a suggested answer** or a **suggested revision** **sends its text at once** as the user's message, with the **same result as the same text typed** and sent. The **draft stays as it was** and is **not sent with it**. The system **does not accept** a suggested answer or revision whose text **no longer fits the chat**, and says that it **no longer applies**.
- **Status:** confirmed
