title: "Disclose material that does not agree with itself"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When **two parts of the material disagree** on a point the work needs, the system **names both parts** and **where each one is**. It **tells the user before the outline** when the disagreement **changes the planned content a lot**. Otherwise it **names the disagreement with the outline**.
- **Status:** confirmed
