title: "Keep a disputed point open in the outline"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the **material disagrees** on a point the outline needs, the system **does not decide which part is correct**. The user is allowed to **choose a source** or to **leave the point unresolved**. A point **left unresolved stays marked as open** in the outline, and its slide shows **no disputed figure as settled**.
- **Status:** confirmed
