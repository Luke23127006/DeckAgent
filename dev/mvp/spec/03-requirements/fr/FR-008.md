## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system **drafts the outline** from the **request, the material, the interview answers and the sources** the user chose, to **fit the topic, audience, duration, purpose and language** of the deck. Each planned slide states **what the slide argues** and gives its **key content**, **keeping the meaning** of the material. The outline keeps a **number of slides the user asks for exactly**, or **states the planned number**, and **states the assumption** used for each **skipped question**.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** Teacher feedback of 23-10-2026: the **length of the deck** follows the **duration of the talk**.
