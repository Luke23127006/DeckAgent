id: FR-025
title: "Keep finished slides when the work on slides ends early"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When **work on slides stops or fails**, the system **keeps each slide it finished** and **shows no unfinished slide**; a slide it did not finish changing **keeps its last finished state**. It says **how many slides it finished or changed**, or that it **does not know**. It says once that the **finished slides were not made to agree** with each other.
- **Status:** confirmed
