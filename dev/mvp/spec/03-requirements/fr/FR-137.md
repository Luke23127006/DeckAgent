id: FR-137
title: "Answer about the deck with what each point rests on"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system answers a question about the deck in the conversation, names the slides the answer is about, and says for each point what it rests on: the deck, the user's material, public research or the model's general knowledge. It uses public research only when the question asks for it. When the deck, the user's material and the sources of the brief do not hold what the answer needs, it says what is missing, marks each content of a kind that needs a source [BR-015] that rests on the model's general knowledge alone as not checked, and never says that it checked what it did not check.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** An answer may use the model's general knowledge when it says so, like the closing messages of the first draft and of a follow-up request.
