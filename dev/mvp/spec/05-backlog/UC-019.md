title: "Carry on changing a deck I exported"
---

## What it is

The user has a deck source that DeckAgent exported, and wants to change it again in
DeckAgent as the same deck, instead of using it as material for a new deck.

## Belongs to

None.

## Why parked

In the spec, an exported source archive attached to a request is material like any
attachment, and nothing is imported as a deck to change.

## Reconsider when

UC-004 (export the deck to a file) or UC-016 (export a session for reading or handover) is
promoted from the backlog.
