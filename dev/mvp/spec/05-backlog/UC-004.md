## What it is

The user wants a **deck outside DeckAgent**: its **source**, to **keep working on it in their own tools**, or **fixed pages or images** to keep, send, print or present. The **deck in the chat stays as it is**.

Requirements only this use case would cite:

- FR-118, **export the source**: **one archive** [BR-012] with the **source of the deck, its assets** and what is needed to use it outside DeckAgent, with **no working record of DeckAgent** and **no saved credential** [NFR-006].
- FR-119, **export the slides as static files**: **PDF with one page per finished slide**, or **one PNG or JPEG image per slide**; **no presenter notes**; **animations do not work**.
- FR-120, **keep content and layout**: the export **changes no slide** and **names each limit of the chosen output** before it gives a file; an output that **loses important information gives no file**.
- FR-121, **export a stable state**: **only when no request is active** and **at least one slide is finished**; the **whole deck in its last finished version**, a **partial deck named as partial**.
- FR-122, **a failed export**: **no incomplete file**, **no change of the deck**, a **new attempt from the state at its start**; an **export calls no model**.
- BR-012, **output kinds**: **ZIP for the source**; **PDF, PNG and JPEG for static files**.
- NFR-015, **visual fidelity**: each page or image **keeps the content, order and layout of its slide** as the deck canvas shows it, with **no text cut off** and **no image missing**.
- The **destination the user chooses** is **FR-085**, **shared with UC-016**.

**Flows to cover**: a request is active; export fails; reload or come back; some content is not kept; the file name is taken; no destination chosen; a partial first draft; no finished slide; a partially changed deck; retry after a failed export.

## Belongs to

None.

## Why parked

**Export is not in the MVP**. **What each generation format supports** for export and for changes by hand **is not decided**.

## Reconsider when

Not set by the PO.

## Findings

- **Each generation format states what it supports**; export gives the **source, PDF or images** and **never changes the format**. A **change of format is a new deck in a new chat** (UC-001), and the **interview says what the format gains and loses**.
- [DEC-006](../../decisions/DEC-006-where-chat-work-is-kept.md) option A **keeps the deck source in DeckAgent's own data**. Its option C, a **copy of the deck source into the project folder on request**, is a **kind of export**.
- **Earlier states of a deck** (UC-008 in the backlog) and **exported files** are **both copies of the deck source**; say **how they relate**.
- A **second deck made from this deck** **attaches an exported file as material**. BR-001 **accepts ZIP but no HTML**, so an **HTML deck is attachable only as a ZIP**.
- **Carrying on with an exported deck** is in the backlog (UC-019); **read it together**.
- Teacher feedback of 23-10-2026 (`dev/meetings/Transcript - 231026 - Họp để thêm các req và các hướng sửa đổi sau V1.txt`, 11:25 to 12:52 and 18:04 to 20:51): **export and editing per generation format**.
