---
id: FR-027
title: "Change the deck within the requested scope"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system applies a clear request to change the deck at the scope it states [NFR-012]. The scope can be named slides, several slides or the whole deck. It changes each such slide in place and builds each new slide at the place the request names. A new slide goes at the end of the deck when the request names no place. It leaves every slide outside the scope as it was, shows no outline and asks for no approval first. A clear request can change the deck a lot, also its topic, purpose or structure. The system then changes this deck. It makes no second deck and drafts no new outline.
- **Updated at:** 25-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Named slide changes in place

- **Given:** A chat whose deck has five slides. No request is active. Slide 3 is about the pilot results.
- **When:** The user sends "make slide 3 shorter".
- **Then:**
  - The system asks no question before it changes slide 3.
  - Slide 3 changes and stays the third slide.
  - Slides 1, 2, 4 and 5 are the same as before the request.
  - The deck has five slides.
  - No outline proposal appears.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · Changed slide follows the instruction

- **Given:** The state of AC-1.
- **When:** The system has changed slide 3.
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 is shorter than before.
  - Slide 3 is still about the pilot results.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · Content the instruction does not concern stays

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. Slide 3 has the title "Results" and three points on the pilot results.
- **When:** The user sends "change the title of slide 3 to 'What we learned'".
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 has the title "What we learned".
  - The three points of slide 3 keep their meaning.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-4 · Whole-deck change without a question

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "make the whole deck more formal".
- **Then:**
  - The system asks no question before it changes the slides.
  - The deck has five slides.
  - No outline proposal appears.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-5 · Whole-deck change follows the instruction

- **Given:** The state of AC-4.
- **When:** The system has changed the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The wording of each slide is more formal than before.
  - Each slide keeps its subject and the facts it gave.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-6 · New slide at the named place

- **Given:** A deck of five slides.
- **When:** The user sends "add a slide on costs after slide 2".
- **Then:**
  - The deck has six slides.
  - The new slide is slide 3.
  - Slides 1 and 2 are the same as before the request.
  - The slides that were 3, 4 and 5 are now 4, 5 and 6, each the same as before.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-7 · Slides outside the scope stay, even when the deck then disagrees

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. Slide 1 lists the titles of slides 2 to 5. Slide 3 has the title "Results".
- **When:** The user sends "change the title of slide 3 to 'What we learned'".
- **Then:**
  - Slide 1 is the same as before the request. It still lists "Results".
  - Slides 2, 4 and 5 are the same as before the request.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-8 · Material given earlier is used

- **Given:** The first request of the chat had the attachment "notes.docx". The notes say that costs fell by 10%. The deck has five slides and none is about costs.
- **When:** The user sends "add a slide on the cost results after slide 4".
- **Then:**
  - The new slide gives the fall in costs from "notes.docx".
- **Check:** judged

### AC-9 · The next request starts from the current deck

- **Given:** The state after AC-3.
- **When:** The user sends "make slide 3 shorter".
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 is shorter than before this request.
  - Slide 3 still has the title "What we learned".
- **Check:** judged

### AC-10 · The confirmed outline informs a later change

- **Given:** A first draft that finished. The confirmed outline plans a talk that builds a case for the hospital board to extend the pilot. The deck has five slides. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "add a closing slide at the end that states our ask".
- **Then:**
  - The new slide asks the hospital board to extend the pilot.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-11 · A large change stays in this deck

- **Given:** A deck of five slides that asks the hospital board to extend the pilot. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "turn the whole deck into a talk that trains new nurses on the pilot steps".
- **Then:**
  - No outline proposal appears.
  - The chat still has one deck. No new chat starts.
  - The slides of the deck show the change.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-12 · New slide with no place named

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "add a slide on costs".
- **Then:**
  - The deck has six slides.
  - The new slide is slide 6.
  - Slides 1 to 5 are the same as before the request, in the same order.
- **Check:** deterministic

## Notes

- The number of conditions is more than the reading guide of seven. They share one purpose: a clear change reaches what the user asked, and nothing else.
- Preservation is checked by comparing the deck before and after the request, not by reading a message of the system.
- A change or a scope that is not clear is covered by FR-030 to FR-035. This holds when it is not clear whether the user wants to replace the deck or to refine it. A slide reference that does not match the deck is covered by FR-036.
