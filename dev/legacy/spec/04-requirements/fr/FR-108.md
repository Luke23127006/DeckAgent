---
id: FR-108
title: "Report design changes that cannot fit content"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When a requested design cannot keep important content and readability within the requested slide structure, the system first makes a presentation that keeps both. When that is not possible, it names the affected slides, explains the conflict and offers to revise the design or to change the content or the structure. It removes no content and adds no slide without the user's instruction. Each affected slide keeps its last finished result until the user chooses.
- **Updated at:** 26-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Content is preserved during a design-only change

- **Given:** A deck has five slides. Slide 3 contains six findings. The user requests a more spacious design for slide 3 while keeping its content.
- **When:** The system determines that the requested design cannot fit all six findings readably within slide 3.
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 keeps its last finished result.
  - The system removes none of the six findings.
  - The system names slide 3 and explains the conflict.
  - The system offers to revise the design or explicitly change the content or slide structure.
  - The request remains unfinished while the system waits for the user's choice.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-2 · An explicit content change is applied

- **Given:** The state of AC-1. The system waits for the user's choice.
- **When:** The user explicitly chooses to keep only the two most important findings on slide 3.
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 contains two findings.
  - The two findings retain their original meaning.
  - Slides 1, 2, 4 and 5 remain unchanged.
  - The conversation identifies the content removed from slide 3.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · A design that keeps the content is tried first

- **Given:** A deck has five slides. Slide 3 gives six short findings. The user sends "make slide 3 calmer and less crowded".
- **When:** The system works on the request.
- **Then:**
  - The system first makes a design that keeps all six findings.
  - The system asks about the content only when no readable design can keep them all.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-4 · No slide is added to make room

- **Given:** A deck has five slides. Slide 3 contains six findings. The user requests a more spacious design for slide 3 while keeping its content.
- **When:** The system determines that the design cannot fit all six findings readably within slide 3.
- **Then:**
  - The deck has five slides.
  - The system adds no slide before the user explicitly chooses to change the slide structure.
- **Check:** deterministic
