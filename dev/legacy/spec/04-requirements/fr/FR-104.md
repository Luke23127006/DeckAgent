---
id: FR-104
title: "Apply independent design rule sets to slides"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system designs each slide from the chosen design system, the chosen visual style when one is chosen, and the content of the slide. A visual style belongs to no design system: the same visual style can be applied with different design systems. A Required Rule holds. A Preferred Rule guides the choice.
- **Updated at:** 26-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · One visual style with two design systems

- **Given:** The visual style "Editorial" is saved. The design systems "Acme" and "Client" are saved. Two chats have no deck.
- **When:** The first deck is built with "Editorial" and "Acme". The second deck is built with "Editorial" and "Client".
- **Then:**
  - Both decks follow the layout and density rules of "Editorial".
  - The first deck follows the brand rules of "Acme".
  - The second deck follows the brand rules of "Client".
  - "Editorial" holds the same rules as before.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-2 · The content shapes each layout

- **Given:** The design system "Acme" and the visual style "Editorial" are chosen. One planned slide gives one figure. Another planned slide gives four findings.
- **When:** The system has built the two slides.
- **Then:**
  - The layout of each slide fits its content.
  - The two slides do not have to share one fixed layout.
  - Each slide shows all of its planned content.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · A Preferred Rule guides and does not block

- **Given:** The design system "Acme" holds a Preferred Rule for a dense layout. The visual style "Editorial" sets an airy layout. A planned slide gives four findings.
- **When:** The system builds the slide.
- **Then:**
  - The system chooses one layout without asking the user to resolve a conflict.
  - The slide shows all four findings.
  - "Acme" and "Editorial" hold the same rules as before.
- **Check:** judged
