id: FR-114
title: "Review and choose an available generation format"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system shows the available generation formats [BR-011], each with a short intended use, and marks the proposed one. The full details of every format are hidden at first. Choosing a format makes it the selected generation format and shows its advantages and important limitations. The details of any other format are hidden. Choosing the selected format again hides its details, and it stays selected. Choosing a format sends no request, answers nothing, confirms nothing, changes no outline and builds no slide.
- **Updated at:** 28-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · First view

- **Given:** A confirmed outline. The system proposed HTML, and Marp and Slidev are also available.
- **When:** The system first shows the generation formats.
- **Then:**
  - Each available format shows a short intended use.
  - The full details of no format are shown.
  - HTML is marked as the proposed format.
  - No format that the system cannot build is shown as available.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · Choosing a format selects it and shows its details

- **Given:** The state of AC-1.
- **When:** The user chooses Marp, and then chooses Slidev.
- **Then:**
  - After the first choice, Marp is the selected generation format, and its advantages and important limitations are shown.
  - After the second choice, Slidev is the selected generation format and its details are shown. Marp is no longer selected, and its details are hidden.
  - At no moment are the details of two formats shown together.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-3 · Choosing the selected format again hides its details

- **Given:** The user chose Slidev, and its details are shown.
- **When:** The user chooses Slidev again.
- **Then:**
  - The details of Slidev are hidden.
  - Slidev is still the selected generation format.
  - Slidev is not confirmed.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-4 · Choosing is not a request or an answer

- **Given:** The request that confirmed the outline waits for the confirmation of the generation format.
- **When:** The user chooses Marp, chooses Slidev, and chooses Slidev again.
- **Then:**
  - No new request starts.
  - The conversation shows no answer of the user to the waiting request.
  - The request still waits for the confirmation of the generation format.
  - The confirmed outline is the same as before.
  - The deck has no slide.
  - The format chosen last is the selected generation format. It is not confirmed.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-5 · Details are relevant to the format

- **Given:** The details of Marp are shown.
- **When:** The user reads them.
- **Then:**
  - The advantages and limitations describe Marp, not another format.
  - They name the limitations that matter for the request.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- Choosing a format is not a suggested option that sends an instruction. FR-013 is not about this choice. The confirmation that ends the wait is FR-115.
