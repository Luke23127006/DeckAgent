---
id: FR-090
title: "Keep private material out of public research"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** Public research does not send private content from the request, conversation, attachments, workspace files or deck to an outside research service. Its search terms contain only public information and no private details from those sources. Permission to search does not grant permission to share private material.
- **Updated at:** 26-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Search a public topic without an internal report

- **Given:** A request includes an internal revenue report and asks for public market trends in 2026. The chat permits public research.
- **When:** The system searches for market trends.
- **Then:**
  - It uses general public search terms about the market and year.
  - It sends no internal revenue figure, private passage, report file or attachment content to the research service.
  - It does not upload the report.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · Research permission is not sharing permission

- **Given:** The chat permits public research and holds private workspace files.
- **When:** The system prepares a public research operation.
- **Then:**
  - It sends no private content from those files to the outside service.
  - It does not treat permission to search as permission to send the files.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-3 · A proposed search would expose private content

- **Given:** A requested comparison involves internal data and public market figures. A proposed public search would include private text from the internal data.
- **When:** The system prepares the search.
- **Then:**
  - It does not send the proposed private text.
  - It uses general public terms instead, where they are enough.
  - It asks for another way to continue when the comparison cannot be completed from permitted information.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- Private content includes nonpublic figures, passages and details from the user's material. Public research requests must also respect NFR-007's existing limit on sending user content outside the selected model provider.
- A request to the user's selected model provider follows NFR-007 and is distinct from a public research connection.
- FR-088 sets which outside research service the system uses. This requirement holds for every research service, approved or not.
- This requirement does not introduce an approval option to upload private material to a research service.
