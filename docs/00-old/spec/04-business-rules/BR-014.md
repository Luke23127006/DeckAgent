title: "Licences of images from public research"
---

## Rule

- **Statement:** The rule sets **which use licences allow an image found by public research** on a slide.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Why:** A **layout can crop or change an image**. An accepted licence must allow the **intended presentation use and those changes**. The system keeps the **source, licence and conditions** with the image.

## Values

### V-1 · Accepted licences

- **Value:** **Closed list**: public domain, CC0, CC BY, and the **Pexels License** for stock photos identified as provided by Pexels and used as part of a presentation under its [licence conditions](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

### V-2 · Licences not accepted

- **Value:** **Every other licence**, including CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC and CC BY-ND.

### V-3 · Evidence and credit

- **Value:** **Source identity and licence evidence** must be retained. **Attribution alone** grants no reuse permission. **Pexels**, **CC0** and **CC BY** remain separate licence values. An unknown author remains explicitly unknown; the system does not invent credit.
