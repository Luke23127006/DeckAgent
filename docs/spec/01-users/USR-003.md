## Who

Schoolteachers in low-resource settings who prepare weekly lessons on a personal phone with an unreliable connection, teach in a language with a non-Latin script, and share the result on paper or through messaging apps. [assumed]

## Needs

| ID | Goal (user's words) | Situation | Use cases | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N-1 | "Have a clear lesson ready each week without spending my evenings on formatting." | I teach several classes. I prepare after school on my phone, usually in one sitting. | none yet | [assumed] |
| N-2 | "Keep going when my connection drops." | Mobile data is slow and cuts out. I cannot redo everything each time. | none yet | [assumed] |
| N-3 | "Give students something they can read from the back of the room and on paper." | A projector is rare. I print for the class or send photos in a group chat. Some students sit far back and one has poor eyesight. | none yet | [assumed] |

| N-5 | "Reuse last week's lesson structure for the next topic." | My lessons follow the same rhythm every week. | none yet | [assumed] |
| N-6 | "Pay nothing, or almost nothing." | Mobile data and tools come out of my own salary. | none yet | [assumed] |

## Knowledge

| Kind | Level | In practice | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domain (the topic they present) | Working | Knows the subject at the level taught. May be less sure at the edge of the curriculum. | [assumed] |
| Presentation craft | None | Has never designed slides and cannot judge readability. Takes what is given. | [assumed] |
| AI / tool literacy | None | Uses messaging and social apps. Has not prompted an AI and may not know it can be told to change things. | [assumed] |
| Language of use (what they write requests in) | Expert | Writes comfortably in the first language. Interface text in another language would be a barrier. | [assumed] |
| Language of the deck and its audience | Expert | Teaches in the same language, with its own script. | [assumed] |

## Context of use

1. Uses a personal phone, mostly in the evening after school; a school computer is rarely available. [assumed]
2. Connection is slow and drops several times in a session; mobile data has a cost. [assumed]
3. Output is printed on a cheap black-and-white printer or sent as photos or files in a messaging group; a projector is rare. [assumed]
4. No brand rules; content is public lesson material; works alone. [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

1. Types lesson text into a notes app and prints plain text. [assumed]
2. Gives up on slides because the tools need a stable connection and a computer. [assumed]
3. Copies a colleague's slides and changes the words, producing broken letters in the script. [assumed]

## Accessibility and situational limits

| Type | Limit | Effect on use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Permanent (user) | None identified: no limit assumed | - | [assumed] |
| Situational (user) | Small screen in bright daylight | Reading and fixing the result is hard on the phone | [assumed] |
| Permanent (audience) | A student with poor eyesight sits at the back | Text must be large and high-contrast | [assumed] |
| Situational (audience) | Students in the back rows cannot read small text; black-and-white printing loses colour distinctions | Readability must not depend on size or colour | [assumed] |

## Special-case hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Affects | Handled by | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-1 | The connection drops during long operations several times a session, and data costs money. Work would have to survive interruption and resume without retyping, and uploads and downloads would have to stay small. | Long-running operations; resume; file sizes | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-2 | All review and fixing happens on a small touch screen. Every step needed to reach a usable result would have to work by touch on a small screen. | Preview; editing; layout | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-3 | Output is printed in black and white on cheap printers. Pages would have to stay readable without colour and without dark backgrounds. | Print-safe output | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-4 | Letters in the user's script may render as broken glyphs or wrap in the wrong place when a font lacks coverage. Generated and delivered text would have to render correctly, including on other people's phones. | Font coverage; output fidelity; language support | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-5 | Lessons repeat weekly in the same structure. The user would need to repeat that structure with new content without describing it again. | Reusing a previous deck; recurrence | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-6 | Students at the back and one with poor eyesight. Minimum text size and contrast would have to hold no matter how much content is on a page. | Accessible output; text density | none yet | [assumed] |
