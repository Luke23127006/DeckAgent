## Who

Final-year university students who must present and defend a thesis to a committee in a language that is not their first, with little time, no income to spend on tools, and no experience building slides. [assumed]

## Needs

| ID | Goal (user's words) | Situation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-1 | "Explain my whole thesis to the committee in 20 minutes without dropping the results that matter." | The defense is in three days. The thesis is about 80 pages. I know the content but not how to shrink it. | [assumed] |
| N-2 | "Be able to answer any question about any number or claim on my slides." | Committee members probe results. A wrong or unexplained number could cost me the grade. | [assumed] |
| N-3 | "Sound clear and confident in English even though it is not my first language." | I present in English. I read slides aloud and stumble on long or unfamiliar words. | [assumed] |
| N-4 | "Finish without paying anything." | I am a student with no income and can only use what is free. | [assumed] |
| N-5 | "Be sure my slides will look right and open on the defense room's computer." | The department supplies that machine and I cannot test on it beforehand. | [assumed] |

## Knowledge

| Kind | Level | In practice | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domain (the topic they present) | Expert | Knows the thesis topic in depth and will notice wrong claims. Does not know what to cut. | [assumed] |
| Presentation craft | Basic | Has made a few class slides. Cannot judge hierarchy or how much text is too much. | [assumed] |
| AI / tool literacy | Working | Uses chat assistants for study and knows prompting basics. May over-trust the output. | [assumed] |
| Language of use (what they write requests in) | Expert | Writes requests fluently in the first language. | [assumed] |
| Language of the deck and its audience | Working | Reads and writes English well enough for a defense but speaks it with effort. | [assumed] |

## Context of use

1. Works on a laptop, at home or in the library, in the evenings over two to three days. [assumed]
2. Presents live from a computer supplied by the department; sends a copy to the advisor beforehand. [assumed]
3. The advisor reviews informally; no formal approval step. [assumed]
4. No money for tools, so free allowances are the only option. [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

1. Copies thesis passages onto slides and ends up with text-heavy slides. [assumed]
2. Asks a chat assistant for slide text, pastes it into a blank presentation tool and fixes the layout by hand. [assumed]
3. Rewrites sentences repeatedly to make them easy to say aloud. [assumed]
4. Hits the free limit of a tool close to the deadline. [assumed]

## Accessibility and situational limits

| Type | Limit | Effect on use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Permanent | None identified: no limit assumed for this segment | - | [assumed] |
| Situational (audience) | A projector in a bright defense room washes out low-contrast slides | Slides must stay readable under poor display conditions | [assumed] |

## Special-case hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Affects | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-1 | A long document (about 80 pages with figures and tables) must be condensed to a fixed talk length (about 20 minutes). The product would have to handle long input and relate the number of slides to speaking time. | Create from a document; input limits | [assumed] |
| H-2 | Every number on a slide will be questioned. Each figure would have to trace back to the thesis, and anything the system adds would have to be distinguishable from thesis content. | Source fidelity; marking added content | [assumed] |
| H-3 | The user thinks in a first language but presents in English. The language of the request may differ from the language of the deck, and wording would have to be simple enough to say aloud. | Language handling; style and reading level | [assumed] |
| H-4 | The defense computer is unknown. The delivered file would have to display correctly where the user's fonts and software are missing. | Output portability; font handling | [assumed] |
| H-5 | Free-only users on a deadline may reach a usage limit the night before. The product would have to keep existing work intact and say plainly what remains and when. | Usage limits; error behavior | [assumed] |
