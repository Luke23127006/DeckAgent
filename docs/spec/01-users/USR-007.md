## Who

- People cannot use the product in their usual way for a limited time. Examples are an injury or a recovery period.
- They have no assistive setup because they did not expect to need one.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Needs

### N-1 · Finish the update this week

- **Goal:** "Finish my team update this week even though I can barely type."
- **Situation:** My dominant wrist is in a cast. Typing long text is slow and tiring.
- **Use cases:** UC-001
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-2 · No special setup

- **Goal:** "Not have to set up special tools just for a few weeks."
- **Situation:** I have no assistive setup. The situation is temporary.
- **Use cases:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-3 · Fix small mistakes cheaply

- **Goal:** "Fix a small mistake without redoing the whole thing."
- **Situation:** Redoing anything costs me effort I do not have right now.
- **Use cases:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Knowledge

- **Domain (the topic they present):** Working. Knows the team's work well.
- **Presentation craft:** Basic. Builds plain updates. Does not design.
- **AI / tool literacy:** Basic. Has tried an AI assistant. Does not know how to use voice input with it.
- **Language of use (what they write requests in):** Expert. Writes fluently, but dictation introduces errors.
- **Language of the deck and its audience:** Expert. Presents to the team in the shared language.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Context of use

- Works at a desktop with one hand.
- May use the operating system's dictation.
- The source is last week's notes, pasted in.
- Presents live to the team in a meeting room.
- The limitation ends in weeks. The person will not invest in setup.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

- Types very little and tries dictation, then spends time fixing recognition mistakes.
- Asks a colleague to type or to adjust slides.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Accessibility and situational limits

### Temporary (user)

- **Limit:** One working hand, reduced typing and mouse use.
- **Effect on use:** The product needs few keystrokes and few precise pointer actions.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Special-case hypotheses

### H-1 · Typing is the scarce resource

- **Situation:** Typing is the scarce resource.
- **Product would have to:** Reach a usable result from short input. Tolerate dictation errors in what the user writes.
- **Affects:** Input effort; tolerance to input errors
- **Handled by:** UC-003.A2
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-2 · No assistive setup

- **Situation:** The user has no assistive configuration and will not set any up.
- **Product would have to:** Make accessible behavior (keyboard operation, large targets) work by default.
- **Affects:** Accessible defaults
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-3 · Small fixes cost effort

- **Situation:** Small fixes cost effort.
- **Product would have to:** Let the user correct one thing without regenerating or risking other parts.
- **Affects:** Scoped changes
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]
