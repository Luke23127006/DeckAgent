## Who

People who cannot use the product in their usual way for a limited time (for example an injury or recovery) and have no assistive setup because they did not expect to need one. [assumed]

| ID | Goal (user's words) | Situation | Use cases | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N-1 | "Finish my team update this week even though I can barely type." | My dominant wrist is in a cast. Typing long text is slow and tiring. | none yet | [assumed] |
| N-2 | "Not have to set up special tools just for a few weeks." | I have no assistive setup and the situation is temporary. | none yet | [assumed] |
| N-3 | "Fix a small mistake without redoing the whole thing." | Redoing anything costs me effort I do not have right now. | none yet | [assumed] |

## Context of use

1. Works at a desktop with one hand, possibly using the operating system's dictation. [assumed]
2. Source is last week's notes, pasted in. [assumed]
3. Presents live to the team in a meeting room. [assumed]
4. The limitation ends in weeks; the person will not invest in setup. [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

1. Types very little and tries dictation, then spends time fixing recognition mistakes. [assumed]
2. Asks a colleague to type or to adjust slides. [assumed]
