## Who

Managers or senior staff who must win a client with a deck within hours, using their company's approved look and rules. They know the business well but rarely build slides. [assumed]

## Context of use

1. Works at a desktop or laptop in the office, with a few hours or less. [assumed]
2. The deck is presented live and then emailed to the client. [assumed]
3. A director reviews before release; legal or marketing may also need to approve. [assumed]
4. The company pays for tools but restricts where client data may be sent. [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

1. Duplicates last quarter's deck and edits by hand, missing leftover names or figures from the previous client. [assumed]
2. Waits for a colleague or agency to fix formatting. [assumed]
3. Avoids pasting client data into public chat tools because of policy, so does the content work by hand. [assumed]
4. Sends reviewers the whole file with no list of what changed. [assumed]
