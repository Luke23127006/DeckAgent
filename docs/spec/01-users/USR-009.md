## Who

Founders and entrepreneurs who must turn an early, still-forming idea into a story others can judge, often without a designer, a team or prior experience of what investors expect. [assumed]

| N-2 | "Find the holes in my idea before the investors do." | I have a meeting in two weeks and no one to challenge me. | none yet | [assumed] |

## Context of use

1. Works at a laptop, mostly alone or with a co-founder, over a couple of weeks. [assumed]
2. Starts with no document: a concept in their head and perhaps a few scribbled notes. [assumed]
3. The deck is emailed to investors beforehand and presented live. [assumed]
4. Tight money; pays for tools only if clearly worth it. [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

1. Stares at a blank deck and does not know the expected structure. [assumed]
2. Copies another startup's deck and rewrites it, which fits poorly. [assumed]
3. Fills gaps with plausible-sounding numbers found online and later cannot source them. [assumed]
4. Asks friends for feedback and gets contradictory advice. [assumed]

## Special-case hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Affects | Handled by | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-1 | There is no source document. The product would have to work from a conversation and draw out the idea through questions, not only transform existing material. | Starting from an idea; clarifying questions | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-2 | The input is vague and may contradict itself. The product would have to say what it assumed and mark what is the founder's claim versus what it filled in. | Marking assumptions; handling vague input | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-3 | A made-up figure is dangerous for credibility. The product would have to avoid inventing numbers, or mark them clearly as placeholders. | Source fidelity; placeholders | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-4 | The user does not know what investors expect. The product would have to offer a conventional structure while leaving room to depart from it. | Structure guidance | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-5 | The story changes after each round of feedback. The user would need to keep earlier versions and compare them. | Version history; comparison | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-6 | The same content serves a reader (email) and a live pitch. The product would have to serve both without the user maintaining two decks. | Output variants; text density | none yet | [assumed] |
| H-7 | An unprotected idea shared with an outside service is a worry for the founder. The product would have to state clearly where the idea goes and who can see it. | Data handling disclosure | none yet | [assumed] |

## Open questions

2. Do founders start from a conversation, or do most bring scribbled notes? Resolved by: team interviews.
