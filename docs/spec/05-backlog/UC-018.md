title: "Rename or delete a chat"
---

## What it is

The user **gives a chat a name** they recognise, or **deletes a chat** they no longer need, with its conversation and its deck.

## Belongs to

None.

## Why parked

It is a **separate goal** from **reopening an earlier chat** (UC-006) and from **workspaces** (UC-013), and it is **not in the MVP**.

## Reconsider when

Not set by the PO.

## Findings

- **open-design documents no rename or delete** of a project or conversation.
