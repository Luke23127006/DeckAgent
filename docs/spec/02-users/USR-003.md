## Who

- Schoolteachers in low-resource settings prepare weekly lessons on a personal phone.
- The phone connection is unreliable.
- They teach in a language with a non-Latin script.
- They share the result on paper or through messaging apps.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Needs

### N-1 · Weekly lesson without formatting work

- **Goal:** "Have a clear lesson ready each week without spending my evenings on formatting."
- **Situation:** I teach several classes. I prepare after school on my phone, usually in one sitting.
- **Use cases:** UC-001
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-2 · Keep going when the connection drops

- **Goal:** "Keep going when my connection drops."
- **Situation:** Mobile data is slow and cuts out. I cannot redo everything each time.
- **Use cases:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-3 · Readable from the back and on paper

- **Goal:** "Give students something they can read from the back of the room and on paper."
- **Situation:** A projector is rare. I print for the class or send photos in a group chat. Some students sit far back, and one has poor eyesight.
- **Use cases:** UC-005 (print file only)
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-4 · Text in my own language

- **Goal:** "See the text in my own language look right."
- **Situation:** I teach in a language with its own script. Other tools show broken letters and misplaced lines.
- **Use cases:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-5 · Reuse last week's structure

- **Goal:** "Reuse last week's lesson structure for the next topic."
- **Situation:** My lessons follow the same rhythm every week.
- **Use cases:** UC-001 (A29, structure only)
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### N-6 · Pay almost nothing

- **Goal:** "Pay nothing, or almost nothing."
- **Situation:** Mobile data and tools come out of my own salary.
- **Use cases:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Knowledge

- **Domain (the topic they present):** Working. Knows the subject at the level taught. May be less sure at the edge of the curriculum.
- **Presentation craft:** None. Has never designed slides and cannot judge readability. Takes what is given.
- **AI / tool literacy:** None. Uses messaging and social apps. Has not prompted an AI and may not know it can be told to change things.
- **Language of use (what they write requests in):** Expert. Writes comfortably in the first language. Interface text in another language would be a barrier.
- **Language of the deck and its audience:** Expert. Teaches in the same language, with its own script.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Context of use

- Uses a personal phone, mostly in the evening after school.
- A school computer is rarely available.
- The connection is slow and drops several times in a session.
- Mobile data has a cost.
- Prints the output on a cheap black-and-white printer, or sends it as photos or files in a messaging group.
- A projector is rare.
- Has no brand rules. The content is public lesson material. Works alone.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Pain points and workarounds today

- Types lesson text into a notes app and prints plain text.
- Gives up on slides because the tools need a stable connection and a computer.
- Copies a colleague's slides and changes the words. The script then shows as broken letters.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Accessibility and situational limits

### Permanent (user)

- **Limit:** None identified: no limit assumed.
- **Effect on use:** None.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### Situational (user)

- **Limit:** Small screen in bright daylight.
- **Effect on use:** Reading and fixing the result is hard on the phone.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### Permanent (audience)

- **Limit:** A student with poor eyesight sits at the back.
- **Effect on use:** Text must be large and high-contrast.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### Situational (audience)

- **Limit:** Students in the back rows cannot read small text. Black-and-white printing loses colour distinctions.
- **Effect on use:** Readability must not depend on size or colour.
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

## Special-case hypotheses

### H-1 · Dropped connection in long operations

- **Situation:** The connection drops during long operations several times a session. Data costs money.
- **Product would have to:** Let work survive an interruption and resume without retyping. Keep uploads and downloads small.
- **Affects:** Long-running operations; resume; file sizes
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-2 · Small touch screen only

- **Situation:** All review and fixing happens on a small touch screen.
- **Product would have to:** Make every step needed to reach a usable result work by touch on a small screen.
- **Affects:** Preview; editing; layout
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-3 · Black-and-white printing

- **Situation:** Output is printed in black and white on cheap printers.
- **Product would have to:** Keep pages readable without colour and without dark backgrounds.
- **Affects:** Print-safe output
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-4 · Script shows as broken glyphs

- **Situation:** Letters in the user's script may show as broken glyphs, or wrap in the wrong place. This happens when a font lacks coverage.
- **Product would have to:** Show generated and delivered text correctly, including on other people's phones.
- **Affects:** Font coverage; output fidelity; language coverage
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-5 · Weekly lessons repeat

- **Situation:** Lessons repeat weekly in the same structure.
- **Product would have to:** Let the user repeat that structure with new content without describing it again.
- **Affects:** Reusing a previous deck; recurrence
- **Handled by:** UC-001.A29 (structure only)
- **Evidence:** [assumed]

### H-6 · Students at the back

- **Situation:** Students sit at the back, and one has poor eyesight.
- **Product would have to:** Hold a minimum text size and contrast, no matter how much content is on a page.
- **Affects:** Accessible output; text density
- **Handled by:** none yet
- **Evidence:** [assumed]
