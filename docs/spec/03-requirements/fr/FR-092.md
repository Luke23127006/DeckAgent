---
id: FR-092
title: "Keep a slide unfinished until known blocking faults are corrected"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system **does not finish a slide** it builds or changes while the slide has **known missing required content**, **wrong facts**, **unreadable content** [NFR-009], or a **colour or a font it chose itself that is not from the theme** of the deck; a **colour or a font the user chose is never such a fault**. It **corrects the fault within the active request**. When it **cannot correct the fault**, it **ends the request unfinished**.

## Notes

- **Colours and fonts are checked against the theme**. The **model corrects its own fault**; **code fixes nothing by itself**.
- The check covers **what the system chose itself**; the **choice of the user wins**.
