id: FR-038
title: "Keep the draft of a chat"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system **keeps the draft of each chat** while the user is **elsewhere in the product**, such as in another chat or in setup, and **shows it unchanged** when the user **comes back**. It also **keeps the draft** when the **interface is reloaded or closed and opened again**, and **every open interface shows the same draft** of a chat. **Going elsewhere adds nothing** to the draft and **sends nothing**. The **draft is not kept after DeckAgent stops**.
