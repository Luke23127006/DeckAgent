---
id: FR-067
title: "Say when material is used only in part"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the system **uses only part of the material** of a request, or material **stops being readable** during the work, it **says so** and **names the material and the part it did not use**, when it knows them. It **never says that it used content it did not use**, and **reads no other file in place of an unreadable one**. When the **missing part changes the result a lot**, it **asks the user to give it again** or to **change the request**.
