title: "Report the change made to the deck"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** **While it changes the deck**, the system says in the conversation **which slides it changes** and **which new slides it builds**. When the work is done, a **closing message names each slide changed or added**, says **what changed in it**, and says that **no slide outside the scope changed**. For the **changed content only**, it also names the **assumptions, the placeholders**, and the content from the **model's general knowledge** or from **public research**, and **never says that it checked** what it did not check.
- **Why:** A **follow-up request discloses its sources** like the first draft, **for the part it changed**.
