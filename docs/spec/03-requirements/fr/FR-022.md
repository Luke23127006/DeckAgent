id: FR-022
title: "Build the slides from the confirmed outline"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** **After the user confirms the outline**, the system **builds the slides from the confirmed outline**, in the **generation format** of the deck. **Each planned slide gives one slide**, **in the order of the outline**, **keeping the meaning** of its planned content. Each slide uses a **layout that fits its planned content** from the **chosen template** of the generation format, or from its **default template** when the user chose none [BR-018], or a **new layout when none fits**, and the slides use the **theme of the chosen design system**, or of the **default design system** when the user chose none [BR-003].

## Notes

- **Code picks the layouts of the template that fit** the planned content of a slide, and the **model chooses among them** and **fills them**. **Only when none fits** does the **model make a new layout**, **within the theme** and **checked like every slide** (FR-092). Each layout states the **kinds of content it serves** and **how much it holds**.
- A generation format has **several built-in templates**; the **user chooses one in the interview** [BR-013]. A user **does not save a template** of their own. There is **no visual style**: the **rules in words of a design system** and the **layouts of the template** take its place.
