id: FR-049
title: "Open an earlier chat"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When the user **opens an earlier chat**, the system **starts no work**, **sends no request**, **changes no chat and no deck**, and **keeps the workspace in use**, also for an **ungrouped chat**. It shows the **conversation of that chat in order** with the **activities of its requests**, the **deck in its last saved state**, and the **selected model** of that chat; when the chat has **no slides yet**, it **says so**. The user is allowed to **send a new request in that chat**.

## Notes

- An **outline that waits for confirmation** and a **first draft that ended early** **keep the behavior of their own FRs**.
- The **draft of each chat stays with that chat**, whole (FR-038).
