---
id: FR-027
title: "Change the deck within the requested scope"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system applies a **clear follow-up request** at the **scope it states**: named slides, marked parts, several slides or the whole deck, **also when the change is large** [NFR-012]. It **changes each slide in scope in place** in the deck source, **builds each new slide** at the **place the request names** or else **at the end of the deck**, and **asks for no approval first**. It **leaves every slide outside the scope** as it was, **drafts no outline** and **makes no second deck**.

## Notes

- A request that **changes the topic or the structure** of the deck **also changes this deck**, with **no outline**.
