---
id: FR-108
title: "Report design changes that cannot fit content"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** When a requested design cannot keep important content and readability within the requested slide structure, the system first makes a presentation that keeps both. When that is not possible, it names the affected slides, explains the conflict and offers to revise the design or to change the content or the structure. It removes no content and adds no slide without the user's instruction. Each affected slide keeps its last finished result until the user chooses.
- **Updated at:** 26-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Content is not removed to fit the design

- **Given:** A deck has five slides. Slide 3 gives six findings. No request is active. The user sends "make the deck minimal, one point on each slide".
- **When:** The system works on the request and finds that one point cannot keep the six findings of slide 3.
- **Then:**
  - The system does not remove findings from slide 3.
  - The system names slide 3 and explains the conflict.
  - The system offers to revise the design, or to change the content or the structure of slide 3.
  - Slide 3 keeps its last finished result.
  - The conversation does not report the request as finished before the user answers.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · An authorized content change is applied

- **Given:** The state of AC-1. The user answers "keep only the two most important findings on slide 3".
- **When:** The request finishes.
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 shows two findings.
  - Slides 1, 2, 4 and 5 are as they were before the request.
  - The conversation names slide 3 as changed and says that findings were removed.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · A design that keeps the content is tried first

- **Given:** A deck has five slides. Slide 3 gives six short findings. The user sends "make slide 3 calmer and less crowded".
- **When:** The system works on the request.
- **Then:**
  - The system first makes a design that keeps all six findings.
  - The system asks about the content only when no readable design can keep them all.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-4 · No slide is added to make room

- **Given:** The state of AC-1.
- **When:** The system works on the request.
- **Then:**
  - The deck has five slides.
  - The system adds no slide before the user chooses to change the structure.
- **Check:** deterministic
