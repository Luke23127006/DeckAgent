---
id: FR-092
title: "Keep a slide unfinished until known blocking faults are corrected"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system does not finish an Agent-created or Agent-changed slide with known missing required content, wrong facts, or unreadable content [NFR-009]. It corrects the problem within the active request. When it cannot correct the problem, it ends the request unfinished.
- **Updated at:** 26-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Known wrong fact does not become a finished slide

- **Given:** A confirmed outline says that revenue increased from 2.4 million to 3.1 million. A result for the planned slide says 4.1 million instead. The system knows this difference before finishing that slide.
- **When:** The system continues working on the slide.
- **Then:**
  - The result with the wrong figure is not shown as a finished slide.
  - The system continues work on the slide within the active request.
  - The system does not report that the draft is complete.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · Corrected result can finish in the same request

- **Given:** The state of AC-1. The system corrects the wrong figure while the request is active.
- **When:** The system finishes the corrected slide.
- **Then:**
  - The slide shows 3.1 million as the final revenue figure.
  - The slide is shown as finished once.
  - No new request or confirmation is needed for this correction.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-3 · Known unreadable result remains unfinished

- **Given:** The system has finished slide 1 of a first draft. The result for slide 2 has important text outside the slide. The system knows this problem and cannot correct it within the request.
- **When:** The request ends.
- **Then:**
  - Slide 1 stays in the deck canvas as it was.
  - Slide 2 is not shown as a finished slide.
  - The conversation says that the request did not finish.
  - The conversation identifies the unreadable content as the reason.
  - The conversation does not say that the draft is ready.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-4 · An unfinished follow-up change keeps the earlier slide

- **Given:** A deck has five slides. The user asks to shorten slides 2 and 3. The system finishes changing slide 2. It knows the changed result for slide 3 cannot be read, and cannot correct it.
- **When:** The request ends unfinished.
- **Then:**
  - Slide 2 keeps its finished change.
  - Slide 3 keeps its last finished state from before the request.
  - Slides 1, 4 and 5 stay as they were.
  - The conversation says that the request did not finish.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-5 · An aesthetic preference is not a blocking fault

- **Given:** A slide preserves its required facts and is readable. It communicates its main message. The user has not asked for a specific visual style.
- **When:** The system finishes the slide in a simple visual style.
- **Then:**
  - The request completes with that slide.
  - The system does not require the user to approve its visual style before completion.
- **Check:** deterministic

## Notes

- These acceptance conditions concern faults that the system knows. They do not state that every possible fault has been detected.
- This requirement adds no retry count, time limit or separate quality-approval step.
- A request that cannot finish is reported under FR-019. Preservation of finished slides is covered by FR-025.
- A new request after a stop or failure follows the existing conversation and recovery rules. The earlier failed request does not restart by itself.
- Display-integrity measurements and their targets remain in NFR-009.
