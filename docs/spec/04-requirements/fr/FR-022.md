---
id: FR-022
title: "Build the slides from the confirmed outline"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** After the user confirms the outline, the system builds the slides of the deck from the confirmed outline. Each slide covers the content of its planned slide. Each planned slide gives one slide, in the order of the outline and with its planned title. An explicit instruction of the user can ask otherwise. The system adds no slide and makes no other change to the structure of the deck by itself. Every slide uses the default design system [BR-003]. The slides use the language of the confirmed outline. The slides keep the meaning of the planned content. A confirmed fact or figure keeps its meaning. No important planned content is dropped. The system chooses the wording, the visual structure and the layout, without changing the approved meaning.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** use case flow; owner decision, 2026-10-13: by default, one planned slide gives one slide, the slides follow the order of the confirmed outline, and the slide titles follow the planned titles; no unsolicited extra slide and no silent change of structure; owner decision, 2026-10-19: the slides keep the meaning of the planned content, no important planned content is dropped, and the agent can choose wording and layout without changing the approved meaning
- **Updated at:** 19-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Slides follow the confirmed proposal

- **Given:** The conversation shows proposal A of four planned slides. Its last planned slide is about next steps. The user sent "replace the slide on next steps with a slide on costs", and the system showed proposal B. The user confirmed B.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The deck has a slide about costs.
  - The deck has no slide about next steps.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-2 · Each slide covers its planned slide

- **Given:** The user confirmed an outline of four planned slides: "Why we ran the pilot", "What we did", "Results" and "Costs". The planned slide "Results" plans fewer errors and faster handover.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - For each planned slide, a slide covers its title and its planned content.
  - The slide on the results covers fewer errors and faster handover.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · One slide for each planned slide

- **Given:** The state of AC-2.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The deck has four slides, as many as the planned slides.
- **Check:** deterministic
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: one planned slide gives one slide by default
- **Why:** The use case builds the slides from the confirmed outline. It does not say that each planned slide gives exactly one slide. This default makes the number of slides testable. Trade-off: the system cannot split a planned slide in two or add a title slide without the user asking.

### AC-4 · Default design system

- **Given:** The state of AC-2. The user chose no design system.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - Every slide uses the default design system [BR-003].
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-5 · A placeholder keeps its missing status

- **Given:** The user confirmed an outline whose point on the cost savings is marked as a placeholder. The notes the user gave have no cost figure.
- **When:** The system has built the slide on costs.
- **Then:**
  - The slide shows that the cost savings are still missing.
  - The slide gives no figure or other fact for the cost savings.
- **Check:** judged
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-12: a point the confirmed outline marks as a placeholder stays missing on its slide; its wording can change, but no fact is invented to fill it
- **Why:** The outline never invents a fact about the user's own subject. This carries the rule to the slides. Trade-off: the user gives the missing fact later.

### AC-6 · Slides in the order of the outline

- **Given:** The state of AC-2.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The slides follow the order of the planned slides.
- **Check:** judged
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: the slides follow the order of the confirmed outline by default
- **Why:** The use case builds the slides from the confirmed outline, but does not say that they keep its order. This default makes the order testable. Trade-off: the system cannot move a planned slide to a place it finds better without the user asking.

### AC-7 · Planned titles become slide titles

- **Given:** The state of AC-2.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - Each title of a planned slide is the title of a slide.
- **Check:** deterministic
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: the slide titles follow the planned titles by default
- **Why:** The use case does not say that the slides keep the titles of the outline. This default lets the user find each planned slide in the deck by its title. Trade-off: the system cannot reword a title to fit the slide without the user asking.

### AC-8 · Slides in the language of the outline

- **Given:** The user confirmed an outline written in Vietnamese. The notes the user gave are in English.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The text of each slide is in Vietnamese.
- **Check:** judged
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: DeckAgent writes deck content in the language the user asks for

### AC-9 · Confirmed figures keep their meaning

- **Given:** The user confirmed an outline. The planned slide on the results plans the figure "errors fell from 40 to 30 a month".
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The slide on the results gives the fall in errors with the same meaning. Examples are "from 40 to 30 a month", "10 fewer a month" or "a 25% fall".
  - The slide gives no other figure for the fall, such as "a 30% fall".
- **Check:** judged

### AC-10 · No content in place of approved content

- **Given:** The user confirmed an outline. The planned slide on the results plans three findings: fewer errors, faster handover and lower cost. The notes give no figure for the cost saving.
- **When:** The system has built the slides.
- **Then:**
  - The slide on the results shows the three findings.
  - The slide puts no other finding in place of one of them.
  - The slide gives no figure for the cost saving.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- Each condition has its own purpose. AC-2 covers coverage: every planned slide has a slide that covers it. AC-9 covers the meaning of a confirmed figure. AC-10 covers content put in place of approved content.
- No slide is built before the confirmation. FR-001 covers it. FR-003 keeps the confirmed outline fixed.
- How the deck canvas shows the slides while they are built is covered by FR-023.
- AC-5 asks for no exact placeholder text. A later request can give the missing fact. FR-027 covers that change.
