title: "Ask which slides an unclear change reaches"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** A request to change the deck can leave unclear which slides the change reaches. The system then says that it cannot tell. It offers two outcomes: apply the change to every slide, or keep the instruction and change no slide now. When the request also asks for a new slide, it also offers to apply the change to new slides only.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** use case flow, and owner decision, 2026-10-08: a request that asks for no new slide is not offered "new slides only"
- **Updated at:** 12-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Two outcomes for an unclear scope

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. Nothing in the conversation says which slides a change of tone reaches. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "make it more formal".
- **Then:**
  - The system says that it cannot tell which slides the change reaches.
  - It offers to apply the change to every slide.
  - It offers to keep the instruction and change no slide now.
  - It does not offer to apply the change to new slides only.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-2 · Third outcome when a new slide is asked for

- **Given:** The state of AC-1 before the request.
- **When:** The user sends "make it more formal and add a slide on costs".
- **Then:**
  - The system offers the two outcomes of AC-1.
  - It also offers to apply the change to new slides only.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- A request whose scope is clear, such as "make the whole deck more formal", gets no question. FR-027 covers it.
- Changing no slide while the system waits is covered by FR-030. The wait and the reply are covered by FR-020.
- The outcomes are covered by FR-032, FR-033 and FR-034.
