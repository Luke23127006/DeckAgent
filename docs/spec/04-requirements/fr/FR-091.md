---
id: FR-091
title: "Make the message of generated slides clear"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system organizes each first-draft or Agent-changed slide around its main message. It makes essential content readable for the intended audience and use [NFR-009]. It chooses a way to present content that fits the purpose, without requiring an image, chart or animation.
- **Updated at:** 26-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Main message of a first draft slide is clear

- **Given:** A confirmed outline plans a slide on financial results. It states that revenue increased while operating costs decreased. The figures are given in the outline.
- **When:** The system finishes the slide.
- **Then:**
  - The slide makes the increase in revenue and the decrease in costs clear.
  - The figures are organized so the reader can follow the comparison.
  - A reader can identify the slide's main message without relying on the closing message.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-2 · A follow-up change communicates the requested message

- **Given:** A deck has a slide on the technical design of a product. The user asks to change that slide for investors, with emphasis on business value.
- **When:** The system finishes changing the slide.
- **Then:**
  - The slide makes the business value clear for the intended audience.
  - The slide no longer makes technical implementation detail the main message.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · A simple slide can communicate clearly

- **Given:** A confirmed outline plans a short closing slide whose message is a clear request for a decision. The user asks for text only.
- **When:** The system finishes the slide.
- **Then:**
  - The slide states the requested decision clearly.
  - The slide uses no image, chart or animation.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-4 · Readable in the intended presentation

- **Given:** The request asks for a presentation to be shown to a classroom. The confirmed outline plans a slide explaining the three main steps of a process.
- **When:** A reader views the finished slide in the intended presentation setting.
- **Then:**
  - The reader can read the three main steps.
  - The reader can tell the order of those steps.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- The accuracy, structure and planned content of the first draft are covered by FR-022.
- The scope and preservation rules for follow-up changes are covered by FR-027.
- Slide display integrity is covered by NFR-009. This requirement adds communication quality, not another display-integrity measurement.
- Choosing a design system, a look or an output format remains covered by their own behavior.
