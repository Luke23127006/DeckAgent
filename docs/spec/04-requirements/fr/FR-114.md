id: FR-114
title: "Review and choose an available generation format"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** The system shows the available generation formats [BR-011], each with a short intended use, and marks the proposed one. The full details of every format are hidden at first. The user can read the advantages and important limitations of one format at a time, hide them again, and choose another format as the candidate. These actions send no request, answer nothing, change no outline and build no slide.
- **Updated at:** 28-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · First view

- **Given:** A confirmed outline. The system proposed HTML, and Marp and Slidev are also available.
- **When:** The system first shows the generation formats.
- **Then:**
  - Each available format shows a short intended use.
  - The full details of no format are shown.
  - HTML is marked as the proposed format.
  - No format that the system cannot build is shown as available.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · One format's details at a time

- **Given:** The state of AC-1.
- **When:** The user asks to read the details of Marp, and then the details of Slidev.
- **Then:**
  - After the first action, the advantages and the important limitations of Marp are shown.
  - After the second action, the details of Slidev are shown, and the details of Marp are hidden.
  - At no moment are the details of two formats shown together.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-3 · Hiding details keeps the choice

- **Given:** The user chose Slidev as the candidate, and its details are shown.
- **When:** The user asks to hide the details of Slidev.
- **Then:**
  - The details of Slidev are hidden.
  - Slidev is still the selected generation format.
  - Slidev is not confirmed.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-4 · Reviewing is not a request or an answer

- **Given:** The request that confirmed the outline waits for the confirmation of the generation format.
- **When:** The user reads the details of two formats, hides them, and chooses another candidate.
- **Then:**
  - No new request starts.
  - The conversation shows no answer of the user to the waiting request.
  - The request still waits for the confirmation of the generation format.
  - The confirmed outline is the same as before.
  - The deck has no slide.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-5 · Details are relevant to the format

- **Given:** The details of Marp are shown.
- **When:** The user reads them.
- **Then:**
  - The advantages and limitations describe Marp, not another format.
  - They name the limitations that matter for the request.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- A choice of format to read about is not a suggested option that sends an instruction. FR-013 is not about this choice. The confirmation that ends the wait is FR-115.
