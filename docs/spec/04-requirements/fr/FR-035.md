---
id: FR-035
title: "Clarify feedback that does not say what to change"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** A request can say that something is wrong without saying what to change, with or without a slide named. The system then offers directions to improve, taken from the deck and the conversation. The user can choose a direction or explain more. Without a direction it can offer, the system says that it cannot tell what to change and asks the user.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** use case flow, and owner decision, 2026-10-11: guided clarification for feedback that does not say what to change, with basic clarification as the fallback
- **Updated at:** 12-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · Directions fit the slide and the conversation

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. Slide 3 has eight long points. The conversation says that the audience is the hospital board. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "Slide 3 doesn't look right".
- **Then:**
  - The system offers directions to improve slide 3.
  - Each direction fits what slide 3 holds or what the conversation says.
  - No direction is about a slide other than slide 3.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-2 · Choose a direction or explain more

- **Given:** The state after AC-1.
- **When:** The user reads the directions.
- **Then:**
  - The user can choose one of the directions.
  - The user can answer in their own words instead.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-3 · Chosen direction leads the change

- **Given:** The state after AC-1. One direction offered is to shorten the points of slide 3.
- **When:** The user chooses that direction.
- **Then:**
  - The points of slide 3 are shorter than before.
  - The system applies no other direction it offered.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-4 · Explanation leads the change

- **Given:** The state after AC-1.
- **When:** The user replies "the board needs the key result first".
- **Then:**
  - Slide 3 shows the key result before its other points.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-5 · Fallback question

- **Given:** A deck of five slides. Each slide is short and follows its planned slide of the confirmed outline. Since the first draft, the user has said nothing about what they like or dislike. No request is active.
- **When:** The user sends "change it", with no slide named and no reason given.
- **Then:**
  - The system says that it cannot tell what to change.
  - The system asks the user what to change.
- **Check:** judged

## Notes

- The directions are not a fixed list. They come from the deck and the conversation.
- Changing no slide before the change is clear is covered by FR-030. The wait and the replies are covered by FR-020. A suggested option sends its instruction as FR-013 says.
- Once the change is clear, it is applied as FR-027 says.
