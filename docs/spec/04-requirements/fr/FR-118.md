---
id: FR-118
title: "Export the current native source"
---

## Requirement

- **Statement:** A source export gives one archive [BR-012] with the current native source of the deck, its assets, and the configuration and dependency information needed to use it outside DeckAgent. It builds and converts nothing again. It keeps presenter notes only when they are part of the native source. It holds no working record of DeckAgent, no unrelated material and no saved credential [NFR-006].
- **Updated at:** 28-10-2026

## Acceptance conditions

### AC-1 · One archive with the native source

- **Given:** A deck of five finished slides in Marp, with two images.
- **When:** The user makes a source export.
- **Then:**
  - The export gives exactly one archive.
  - The archive holds the Marp source of the five slides and both images.
  - The archive holds the configuration and dependency information the source needs.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-2 · Usable without DeckAgent

- **Given:** The archive of AC-1.
- **When:** A person opens the source with the tool its format needs, without DeckAgent.
- **Then:**
  - The five slides show with their text and images.
  - The source needs no part of DeckAgent to run.
- **Check:** judged

### AC-3 · Notes only where the source holds them

- **Given:** Two decks. In the first, presenter notes are part of the native source of slides 1 and 2. The second has no notes.
- **When:** The user makes a source export of each deck.
- **Then:**
  - The first archive keeps the notes of slides 1 and 2.
  - The second archive has no notes.
  - The export makes no new note.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-4 · No unrelated material or credential

- **Given:** A chat with a deck, two attachments, an outline and a saved key of a provider configuration.
- **When:** The user makes a source export.
- **Then:**
  - The archive holds no attachment that the deck does not use.
  - The archive holds no outline, conversation or working record of DeckAgent.
  - The archive holds no saved key [NFR-006].
  - An image from an attachment that a slide uses is in the archive.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-5 · Nothing built again

- **Given:** A deck of five finished slides.
- **When:** The user makes a source export.
- **Then:**
  - No model is called.
  - The deck and its generation format are the same as before.
- **Check:** deterministic

### AC-6 · A missing source file fails the export

- **Given:** A deck of five finished slides. One image that slide 2 needs cannot be read when the archive is made.
- **When:** The user makes a source export.
- **Then:**
  - No archive is given.
  - The system says that the export failed, and names the file it could not read.
  - The system does not call the result a partial deck.
- **Check:** deterministic
