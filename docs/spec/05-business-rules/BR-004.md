title: "Chats kept as earlier work"
---

## Rule

- **Statement:** The rule sets which chats the system keeps as earlier work that the user can open. It also sets how long it keeps them, and which chats stay reachable when a workspace is removed.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** use case flow, and owner decision, 2026-10-11: earlier chats and their decks stay available after DeckAgent is closed and opened again; owner decision, 2026-10-13: a chat with no sent request need not appear among earlier chats; saved chats, their conversations and their decks are kept with no deletion based on time; owner decision, 2026-10-25: removing a workspace removes no folder, no file and no chat, and the chats of the removed workspace stay reachable
- **Updated at:** 25-10-2026

## Values

### V-1 · Which chats are kept

- **Value:** Every chat in which the user sent at least one request. A chat with no sent request is not required to appear.
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: a chat with no sent request need not appear among earlier chats

### V-2 · How long chats are kept

- **Value:** No time limit. The system deletes no chat, conversation or deck because of its age.
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: saved chats, their conversations and their decks are kept with no deletion based on time; closing and opening DeckAgent again sets no end to the time they are kept

### V-3 · Chats of a removed workspace

- **Value:** Every chat of a workspace stays reachable after that workspace is removed. Removing a workspace changes no folder, no file, no chat, no conversation and no deck on the computer.
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-25: removing a workspace removes no folder, no file and no chat; the chats of the removed workspace stay reachable

## Notes

- Until 2026-10-13, V-1 was pending, and an open item asked whether chats are kept only for a limited time. The owner decisions of 2026-10-13 settled both.
- No time limit is not a promise against loss that DeckAgent does not cause. Examples are files the user removes on purpose and a lost disk.
- This rule adds no way to delete a chat or a deck.
- V-3 does not say where the chats of a removed workspace appear. The use case that adds and removes workspaces names one example and leaves the decision open.
