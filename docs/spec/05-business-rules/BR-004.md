---

## Rule

- **Statement:** The rule sets which chats the system keeps as earlier work that the user can open. It also sets how long it keeps them.
- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** use case flow, and owner decision, 2026-10-11: earlier chats and their decks stay available after DeckAgent is closed and opened again; owner decision, 2026-10-13: a chat with no sent request need not appear among earlier chats; saved chats, their conversations and their decks are kept with no deletion based on time
- **Updated at:** 13-10-2026

## Values

### V-1 · Which chats are kept

- **Value:** Every chat in which the user sent at least one request. A chat with no sent request is not required to appear.
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: a chat with no sent request need not appear among earlier chats

### V-2 · How long chats are kept

- **Value:** No time limit. The system deletes no chat, conversation or deck because of its age.
- **Source:** owner decision, 2026-10-13: saved chats, their conversations and their decks are kept with no deletion based on time; closing and opening DeckAgent again sets no end to the time they are kept

## Notes

- Until 2026-10-13, V-1 was pending, and an open item asked whether chats are kept only for a limited time. The owner decisions of 2026-10-13 settled both.
- No time limit is not a promise against loss that DeckAgent does not cause. Examples are files the user removes on purpose and a lost disk.
- This rule adds no way to delete a chat or a deck.
